Privacy Policy

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

If you choose to contact us via email, any information you provide—such as your name, email address, or other details—will be used solely for the purpose of responding to your message. We do not retain or store this information beyond what's necessary for the reply, and it will be promptly deleted unless there's a specific reason to keep it (such as ongoing support or legal requirements).

We do not use tracking technologies, analytics tools, or third-party data processors.

Your privacy matters, and we believe in keeping things simple and respectful. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to get in touch.
