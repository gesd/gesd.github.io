About Guitari

The idea began with trying to remember a chord progression without having a guitar nearby and wondering whether a phone screen could work as a fretboard. That thought led to a conversation with an experienced guitar teacher who immediately saw the potential, and the concept grew into a tool shaped by real musical experience rather than just a slick interface. Every small detail is designed to support genuine understanding and muscle memory, so time spent training on the app translates directly to better playing on a real guitar. This approach will continue to guide new features and refinements as the app evolves.

Why Guitari?

  • Practice chord shapes, scales, and chord recognition on the go.
  • Use Free Play mode to explore melodies when you don’t have your guitar.
  • Identify chords by forming shapes directly on your screen.
  • Optimised for both iOS and Android devices.
  • Designed for fast learning and real musical confidence.
