Account & Data Deletion Request

You have the right to request the deletion of your TinyDoors account and all associated data.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Upon processing your request, the following information will be permanently deleted from our servers:

  • Your account details (Name, Email, Profile Picture from your sign-in provider).
  • All content you have created and stored in the app, such as calendars, entries, and any associated media.
  • Any links between you and calendars you have sent or received.

Please note that anonymized crash and analytics data, which cannot be tied to your personal account, will not be deleted.

How to Request Deletion

To initiate the deletion process, please click the button below. This will open your email client with a pre-filled message. You must send this email from the address associated with your TinyDoors account to verify your identity.

You can add your Firebase User ID (UID) to the email body to speed up the process. You can find this in the app's settings screen under "Account Info".

Request Account Deletion

We will process your request within 14 days and send a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.
