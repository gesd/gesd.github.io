Privacy Policy — TinyDoors

Effective Date: 28th November 2025

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

  1. Information You Provide to Us:

    Account information (e.g., name, email, profile picture) if you sign in (for example via Google Sign-In). User content you voluntarily create and store in the App (e.g., door descriptions, journal entries, uploaded photos).

  2. Information Collected Automatically:

    We use third‑party services to collect device and usage data:

    • Usage Data: Firebase Analytics collects aggregated interaction data (features used, pages viewed, time spent).
    • Crash & Performance: Firebase Crashlytics collects crash traces and device state to help fix bugs.
    • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and unique device identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to operate and improve the App, authenticate users, store user data, respond to support requests, and develop new features.

3. Data Storage

Your app data (for example, journal entries or other user content) is stored securely using Google Firebase services to provide core functionality and, if applicable, sync across devices.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell personal information. We share data only in limited circumstances:

  • With third‑party service providers (Google Firebase) whose use of data is governed by their privacy policies.
  • When required by law or to comply with valid legal requests.

5. Your Data Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the email below. If the App offers an account deletion feature, you may be able to delete data from within the app.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no electronic storage or transmission is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if discovered, we will take steps to delete it.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in the App or on our website — please review periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: samdek-app-support@googlegroups.com.
